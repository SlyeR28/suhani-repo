# 📸 Girlfriend Photos Folder

Place your girlfriend's photos in this directory!

## Supported Files:
- `photo1.jpg` — Chapter 1: Where our story began
- `photo2.jpg` — Chapter 2: Her radiant smile
- `photo3.jpg` — Chapter 3: Favorite memories
- `photo4.jpg` — Chapter 4: The Grand Proposal climax

You can replace any of these files with your own JPG or PNG images (just keep the filenames `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, `photo3.jpg`, `photo4.jpg` or update them in `ProposalController.java`).
